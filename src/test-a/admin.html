Add account

Creates the account, then opens a Chromium window on the onboarding site for you to log in. The session is captured when you press Done.

Add proxies

One per line, as host:port:username:password (the username may carry provider parameters), host:port, or http://user:pass@host:port. Duplicates are skipped. Passwords are encrypted on save and never displayed again.
