Account & session management

Each account holds one saved, encrypted onboarding session. Workflows use these sessions and save the refreshed session back on the same account after a successful run. Once an applicant opens their role link the account is held for your review until you release it or mark it verified; a verified account is taken by that applicant and never used for anyone else. Cookie values are never shown.

Proxy egress …

Each proxy is bound to one account the first time that account runs (or opens a login browser) and is reused by that account from then on, so the provider never sees the account on a new session. After a run it is held for its account. Release proxy clears the binding and returns it to the unused pool: do that only after confirming with the provider that the session can be reused. Down or retired proxies keep their account until restored. Credentials are stored encrypted and never shown.

Add account

Creates the account, then opens a Chromium window on the onboarding site for you to log in. The session is captured when you press Done.

Add proxies

One per line, as host:port:username:password (the username may carry provider parameters), host:port, or http://user:pass@host:port. Duplicates are skipped. Passwords are encrypted on save and never displayed again.
